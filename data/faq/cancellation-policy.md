# Cancellation and rescheduling policy

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## How to cancel
You can cancel an appointment through this assistant with your booking ID, or at the reception
desk. Cancelling frees the slot so another patient can use it.

## Cancellation fee
There is no fee for cancelling. We do ask you to cancel at least two hours before your appointment
so the slot can be offered to someone else.

## Rescheduling
To move an appointment, cancel the old booking and book a new slot. The assistant can show the
free slots for any day in the next week.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the doctor may see you after the patients who arrived on
time, or we may ask you to book a new slot.

## Missed appointments
If you miss an appointment without cancelling, you can still book again. After three missed
appointments in a row, reception will call you to confirm future bookings.

## Refunds
Consultation fees are paid at the visit, so there is nothing to refund when you cancel online.
Fees already paid at the counter can be refunded at the billing counter within 7 days.
