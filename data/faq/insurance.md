# Insurance and payment

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## Accepted insurance
We accept cashless treatment with the (fictional) insurers SunCare Health, BlueLeaf Insurance and
Trident Mediclaim. For other insurers, pay at the clinic and claim the amount back from your insurer
with the bills and reports we provide.

## What to bring for cashless treatment
Bring your insurance card and a photo ID. Our insurance desk checks your policy before the
consultation. Please arrive 15 minutes early so the check does not delay your appointment.

## Consultation fees
A consultation costs Rs 500 in General Medicine and Rs 700 in Orthopedics, ENT and Pediatrics.
A follow-up visit within 7 days with the same doctor is free.

## Ways to pay
You can pay by cash, debit or credit card, or UPI at the billing counter on the ground floor.
We do not take payments over chat or phone.

## Bills and receipts
You get a printed receipt after every payment. Lost receipts can be reprinted at the billing counter
with your booking ID.
