# Visiting hours

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## General visiting hours
Family and friends can visit patients in the observation ward every day from 10:00 to 12:00 and
from 16:00 to 19:00. Outside these hours the ward is closed to visitors so patients can rest.

## How many visitors
Two visitors may be at a bedside at the same time. One family member may stay overnight with a
patient if the ward nurse agrees. Children under 12 must be with an adult at all times.

## Children's ward
Parents and guardians can be with their child at any time, day or night. Other visitors follow the
general visiting hours.

## Visitor rules
Please sign in at the reception desk and wear your visitor badge. Keep your phone on silent in the
wards. Visitors with a cold, cough or fever are asked to postpone their visit. Outside food for
patients should be checked with the ward nurse first.

## Clinic opening hours
The outpatient clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00, and closed on Sundays and public
holidays. Appointments run from 10:00 to 17:00.
