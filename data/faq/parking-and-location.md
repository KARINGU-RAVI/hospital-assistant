# Parking and location

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## Address
Sunrise Clinic, 12 Lakeview Road, Green Park, Hyderabad 500001 (a made-up address).
The clinic is opposite Green Park bus stop.

## Getting here
Buses 21 and 47 stop at Green Park, a two-minute walk away. The nearest metro station is Lakeview,
about 800 metres from the clinic. Auto-rickshaws and cabs can drop you at the main entrance.

## Parking
There is a basement car park with 40 spaces, open from 08:30 to 19:30. Parking is free for the first
two hours for patients and visitors; show your appointment slip at the exit. Two-wheeler parking is
next to the main gate.

## Accessibility
The main entrance has a ramp, and wheelchairs are available at reception. The lift reaches every
floor. Two parking spaces near the lift are reserved for people with disabilities.

## Drop-off
A drop-off point at the main entrance lets you leave a patient at the door before parking.
Please do not wait there for longer than five minutes.
