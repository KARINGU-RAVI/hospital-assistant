# Emergencies and contact

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## In an emergency
Sunrise Clinic has no emergency department, maternity ward or operating theatre. In an emergency,
such as chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, a seizure or someone who is unconscious,
call 108 for an ambulance or go to the nearest hospital emergency room straight away.
The nearest hospital with a 24-hour emergency room is Green Park City Hospital (fictional),
about 2 km from the clinic on Lakeview Road.

## Clinic hours
The outpatient clinic is open every day from 09:00 to 18:00, including weekends.
Appointments run from 10:00 to 17:00. The clinic is closed on public holidays.

## How to contact us
You can book, view or cancel appointments any time through this assistant.
For other questions, email the front desk at frontdesk@sunriseclinic.example or visit our
website, www.sunriseclinic.example. We reply to emails within one working day.
We do not give medical advice by email.

## Visiting us
The clinic is at 12 Lakeview Road, Green Park. See the parking and location page for directions.
