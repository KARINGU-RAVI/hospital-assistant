# Departments

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## General Medicine
General Medicine is the first stop for fever, cold, general check-ups and anything that does not
clearly belong to another department. Our doctors can refer you to a specialist if needed.
Doctors: Dr. Sharma and Dr. Iyer.

## Orthopedics
Orthopedics looks after bones, joints, muscles, back pain and sports injuries.
Doctors: Dr. Rao and Dr. Kapoor.

## ENT
The ENT (ear, nose and throat) department sees patients with ear, nose, sinus and throat concerns.
Doctor: Dr. Meena.

## Pediatrics
Pediatrics cares for children up to 14 years old. Doctor: Dr. Fernandes.

## Where to find each department
General Medicine and Pediatrics are on the ground floor. Orthopedics and ENT are on the first floor.
The lift is next to the main reception.

## Not available here
Sunrise Clinic has no emergency department, maternity ward or surgery theatre. In an emergency,
call 108 or go to the nearest emergency room.
