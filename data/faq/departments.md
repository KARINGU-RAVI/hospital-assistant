# Departments

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## General Medicine
General Medicine is the first stop for fever, cold, headaches, general check-ups and anything that
does not clearly belong to another department. Our doctors can refer you to a specialist if needed.

## Orthopedics
Orthopedics looks after bones, joints, muscles, back pain and sports injuries.

## ENT
The ENT (ear, nose and throat) department sees patients with ear, nose, sinus and throat concerns.

## Pediatrics
Pediatrics cares for children up to 14 years old.

## Our doctors
Ask the assistant for the current list of doctors in each department. It always uses the clinic's
live doctor and appointment records.

## Where to find each department
General Medicine and Pediatrics are on the ground floor. Orthopedics and ENT are on the first floor.
The lift is next to the main reception.
