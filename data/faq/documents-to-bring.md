# Documents to bring

Sunrise Clinic is a fictional clinic. All details below are made up for a learning project.

## First visit
Bring a photo ID (Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence or voter ID) and your booking ID.
Reception registers you as a new patient, which takes about five minutes.

## Earlier reports and prescriptions
Bring any earlier test reports, scans, discharge summaries and current prescriptions, even from
other hospitals. The doctor can see your history more quickly that way.

## Insurance papers
For cashless treatment, bring your insurance card and policy number. See the insurance page for
the insurers we work with.

## Children
For a child's appointment, bring the child's birth certificate or Aadhaar card, the vaccination
card, and the parent's or guardian's photo ID.

## Follow-up visits
For a follow-up, bring the receipt or prescription from your last visit and your booking ID.

## Forgot something?
You can still see the doctor without earlier reports. Reception can also take digital copies on
your phone and add them to your file.
